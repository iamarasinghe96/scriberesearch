# Finding title

Status: hypothesis / observed / validated / resolved

## Scope and date

Exact authorized host and UTC observation time. Reference the private approval
record by identifier only.

## Observation and evidence

Describe what was directly observed. Include only sanitized evidence and an
approved private evidence reference where needed.

## Reproduction

Document the minimum approved, non-destructive steps and tool version/commit.

## Impact and confidence

Explain demonstrated impact, affected assumptions, and uncertainty. Do not
assign severity solely from an absent header or a speculative attack chain.

## Recommendation and retest

Describe a practical remediation and an approved verification method.
